    Privacy Policy

    Effective November 3, 2026 · Last updated November 3, 2026

    The short version

    • Cadence runs on your computer. Your videos, captions and schedule stay there.
    • Cadence sends data to TikTok only when you post, and only through TikTok's official API.
    • We don't run analytics, show ads, or sell or share your information.
    • Sign out or uninstall to remove what Cadence stores.

    Who we are

    Cadence ("Cadence", "we", "us") is a desktop application published by [Developer or company name]. This policy explains what information Cadence handles when you use it to upload and schedule videos to TikTok, and the choices you have.

    Cadence is an independent product. It is not made, endorsed or sponsored by TikTok or ByteDance.

    Information Cadence handles

    Information you provide

    • Video files you choose to validate, upload or save as drafts.
    • Post details: captions, hashtags and mentions, visibility setting, comment, Duet and Stitch settings, content disclosure choices, AI-generated content label, cover frame and scheduled time.
    • Draft notes you add in Cadence's draft library.

    Information from TikTok, with your permission

    When you sign in with TikTok you choose which permissions to grant. Cadence requests these:

    PermissionWhat Cadence receives or does
    user.info.basicYour TikTok open ID, display name and profile picture link, to show which account is signed in.
    video.publishYour account's posting options (username, nickname, allowed visibility settings, interaction settings, longest allowed video), and the ability to post videos you approve.
    video.uploadThe ability to send videos you choose to your TikTok inbox as drafts.

    Cadence also receives an access token, a refresh token and the processing status of each post (for example "processing" or "published").

    Information Cadence creates

    • Technical details of your videos read with FFmpeg (resolution, frame rate, codecs, duration, size) and a SHA-256 fingerprint used to confirm the file hasn't changed before upload.
    • Your queue: file locations, post status, TikTok publish IDs, error messages and retry counts.
    • App settings such as your theme and scheduling preferences.

    Cadence does not collect your TikTok password, contacts, location, device identifiers, payment information, or usage analytics.

    How Cadence uses information

    • To check your videos against TikTok's technical requirements.
    • To upload videos and post details to TikTok when you press Post, or at the time you scheduled.
    • To show your account, posting options and the status of each post.
    • To keep you signed in by renewing your access token.

    Cadence does not use your information for advertising, profiling or training machine-learning models.

    Where information is stored

    Cadence stores information on your computer only:

    WhatWhere
    TikTok sign-in tokensYour operating system's secure credential store (macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager, or Secret Service on Linux). If none is available, a file readable only by your user account in the Cadence data folder.
    Queue, drafts and post detailsA local database file in the Cadence data folder.
    SettingsYour operating system's standard settings store.
    Video filesWhere you keep them. Cadence reads them and does not copy them, except when you choose a conversion, which writes a new file next to the original.

    Cadence data folder: %APPDATA%\Cadence on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/Cadence on macOS, and ~/.local/share/Cadence on Linux.

    When information is shared

    With TikTok. When you post or send to your inbox, Cadence transmits the video file and post details directly to TikTok's servers over HTTPS. TikTok's handling of that content is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

    With our sign-in service, if your version uses one. Some versions of Cadence complete TikTok sign-in through a small token service operated by [Developer or company name], so the app's secret key is not stored on your computer. That service receives the one-time authorization code or your refresh token, passes it to TikTok, and returns the result to Cadence. It does not store tokens, and its logs do not record request contents.

    When required by law. Because Cadence keeps your information on your computer, we generally have nothing to disclose. If we ever hold information about you, we may disclose it if required by law or to protect against fraud or abuse.

    We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

    How long information is kept

    • Tokens are kept until you sign out. Signing out asks TikTok to revoke them and deletes them from your computer.
    • Queue entries and drafts are kept until you remove them in Cadence or delete the Cadence data folder.
    • Converted files stay on your computer until you delete them.

    Your choices and rights

    • Revoke access by signing out in Cadence, or by removing Cadence from the apps connected to your TikTok account in TikTok's settings.
    • Delete your data by removing items in Cadence, signing out, and deleting the Cadence data folder listed above.
    • Decline permissions on TikTok's sign-in screen. Cadence will explain which features need them.

    Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or state privacy laws in the United States such as the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, and to object to or restrict its processing. Most of this information is already under your control on your device. For anything we hold, contact us using the details below and we will respond within the time the law requires. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

    Where the GDPR applies, we process information to provide the service you ask for (performance of a contract) and, for the optional sign-in service, our legitimate interest in keeping app credentials secure.

    Security

    Cadence signs in using TikTok's OAuth flow with PKCE and a check against cross-site request forgery, uses HTTPS for every request to TikTok, stores tokens in your operating system's credential store, and verifies each file's fingerprint before upload. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so keep your computer and operating system account protected.

    Children

    Cadence is not directed to children under 13, and you must meet TikTok's minimum age requirements to use TikTok. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used Cadence, contact us.

    Changes to this policy

    We will update this page when Cadence's handling of information changes, and revise the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
